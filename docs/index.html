Your whole library, one controller, zero desktop.

LiftOff is a free, gamepad-first launcher for Windows handhelds. Steam, Game Pass, GOG, Epic, Battle.net, and cloud games land in one library you drive from the couch.

One library for every store you already buy from.

LiftOff finds your games in Steam, Game Pass and the Microsoft Store, GOG, Epic, and Battle.net, adds cloud games and your desktop apps, and puts them all behind one grid with cover art.

Built for a controller in your hands.

Every screen works with a thumbstick. Nothing needs a keyboard or a mouse.

Gamepad-native

D-pad and stick navigation, bumper tab switching, hold-to-repeat, and on-screen button hints everywhere. Built and tested on a ROG Ally X.

Your full Steam libraryNEW

Sign in by scanning a QR code with your phone. Every game you own shows up, including the ones you haven't installed yet, with playtime and last played.

Install Game Pass gamesNEW

Start Game Pass and Microsoft Store installs from inside LiftOff, see free space before you commit, and watch progress without opening the Store.

Cloud gamesNEW

Pick from the cloud catalog and launch titles full screen, right next to the games installed on your device.

Music while you playNEW

Spotify controls live on Home. Choose a playlist, pick the device it plays on, and keep a now-playing bar in view.

Game detailsNEW

One press opens a game's art, description, screenshots, last-played time, and Steam Deck rating, with Play, Install, or Manage ready to go.

Artwork that fills the screen

Covers, hero banners, and logos come from SteamGridDB automatically, animated heroes included. Replace any match you don't like.

Make it yoursNEW

Twelve animated themes, twelve accent colors, and seven surface styles, in any combination you like.

Set up in a minuteNEW

First launch walks you through language, look, stores, and accounts with your controller, then drops you on a full library.

Make it look like yours.

LiftOff 2.0 ships with twelve animated themes, twelve accent colors, and seven surface styles. Pick a theme and an accent to see them on the Games tab.

Themes
Accents

Seven surface styles

  • GlassFrosted, blurred panels that let the theme show through.
  • AeroGlossy glass with a bright highlight along the top.
  • MaterialSolid, paper-like cards with soft shadows.
  • ClearMinimal panels that keep the focus on your artwork.
  • ObsidianSmoked dark glass with warm edge highlights.
  • NeonThin outlines that glow in your accent color.
  • Win9XBevelled grey windows straight out of 1998.
