Frequently Asked Questions

Can you play Halo Infinite on cloud gaming?

Yes, Halo Infinite is available on cloud gaming on Xbox Cloud, via BYOG on Boosteroid, GeForce Now and Xbox Cloud, and on Cloud PCs like Shadow, airgpu and CloudDeck.

On Xbox Cloud, an active subscription is all you need to stream Halo Infinite instantly. No separate game purchase is required.

Alternatively, on BYOG ("Bring Your Own Game") services, you access the game through a supported store or launcher account linked to the service. Since the game is free-to-play, no game purchase is required there either.

Cloud PC services let you install and play it in the cloud just like on a regular PC.

Which cloud gaming services support Halo Infinite?

Halo Infinite is supported on 6 cloud gaming services. These are the plans where it is available:

  • Shadow: Any Plan
  • airgpu: Any Plan
  • CloudDeck: Any Plan
  • Boosteroid: Any Plan
  • GeForce Now: Any Plan
  • Xbox Cloud: Any Plan

Availability may change over time as providers update their catalogs.

Do I need to buy Halo Infinite for cloud gaming?

No, you do not need to buy Halo Infinite for cloud gaming because it is a free-to-play game.

If you use a service and plan that includes the game, you can launch it directly. On BYOG services, you may need to add it to a supported store library to link it to your account. On Cloud PC platforms, you have to install it yourself. Either way, no game purchase is necessary.

Can you play Halo Infinite in your browser?

Yes, you can play Halo Infinite in your browser via cloud gaming on services such as Shadow, Boosteroid, GeForce Now and Xbox Cloud.

These platforms let you launch the game directly from a web browser without installing anything on your device.

The other available services, airgpu and CloudDeck, do not support browser play and require an app.

On which devices is Halo Infinite available via cloud gaming?

Halo Infinite is available via cloud gaming on Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS, Android, iOS, smart TVs, handhelds, and many other devices.

This works through browser-compatible services like Shadow, Boosteroid, GeForce Now and Xbox Cloud, where you start the game directly in the browser. Most providers also offer native apps across a wide range of devices.

airgpu and CloudDeck do not offer direct browser streaming or their own app. Instead, you use remote desktop apps such as Parsec or Moonlight to play on devices that support these apps.

Can you play Halo Infinite for free on cloud gaming?

Yes, you can play Halo Infinite for free on cloud gaming through the GeForce Now free tier.

Since the game is free-to-play, no purchase is required, but you may need to add it to a supported store library and link it to your GeForce Now account.

Is multiplayer for Halo Infinite supported on cloud gaming?

Yes, multiplayer for Halo Infinite is supported on cloud gaming.

Once you launch it through a supported cloud gaming service, you can play online just like on a regular PC or console.

Cross-play availability depends on the game.
